PRIVATE WORKS SYSTEM PROPOSAL

🌐 Private Works: A WebApp that connects citizens, companies and the State for public infrastructure projects.

📌 Citizen Proposals: Citizens propose Public Works projects and citizens vote on them.

💰 Private Investments: Companies with accounts on the platform can finance approved projects. The more financing proposals a project receives, the more competitive the selection process will be.

🏛️ Call for Tender: The State chooses the best proposal among those submitted, ensuring quality and cost optimization.

🔒 Transparency: Data protection and regulatory compliance that guarantee a fair, visible and corruption-free process.

💡 Objective: Promote citizen participation and competition in the financing of Public Works, improving quality and at a lower cost.
